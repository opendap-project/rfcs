## Working here

- Record only what the maintainers proposed or confirmed. Do not invent motivation, alternatives or decisions.
- Follow the process in `README.md`; start from `0000-template.md`. The maintainers decide acceptance.
- When an RFC is accepted, record the outcome in each affected repository with `/opendap:requirement` and, for decisions, `/opendap:adr`.
